## RU — Описание

Это **не коммерческий проект «ДОМА»**. Это учебный постер магистерского семинара «Sustainable Architecture, Urban and Landscape Planning» (Технический университет Мюнхена, TUM, Institute of Energy Efficient and Sustainable Design and Building, руководитель — Prof. Dr.-Ing. ### Что это
Комплексная оценка устойчивости города Кампала (Уганда) по 6 направлениям: городское планирование, зелёная инфраструктура, мобильность, энергия, вода, отходы. По каждому направлению — сводный рейтинг (шкала 0–5, на основе международных бенчмарков по 18 индикаторам), диагностика текущей ситуации и предлагаемые цели/действия.

### Данные
Все цифры на странице — из рабочего файла авторов постера (`Templates_Poster_Graphs_WS21_v1.xlsx`, версия от 08.11.2021) и итогового постера (`Poster_20.02.2022_Final_Version.pdf`):
- Демографические и страновые показатели (население, плотность, доход, грамотность, продолжительность жизни, экологический след)
- Рейтинг устойчивости — 18 индикаторов по 6 направлениям, шкала 0–5
- Половозрастная структура населения (2019)
- Зелёная/синяя инфраструктура (2022)
- Структура транспортных передвижений (2008)
- Источники первичной энергии (2014)
- Источники водоснабжения (2016)
- Способы утилизации отходов (2016)

Полный список из 10 первичных источников — по QR-коду на оригинальном постере (не входит в этот HTML).

## EN — Description

### ⚠️ Case Origin — Please Read First
This is **not a commercial "DOMA" project**. It is an academic poster from the master's seminar "Sustainable Architecture, Urban and Landscape Planning" (Technical University of Munich, TUM, Institute of Energy Efficient and Sustainable Design and Building, led by Prof. Dr.-Ing. Werner Lang, Winter Semester 2021/22).

Original poster authors: **Yehia Abdelazziz, Ahmed Genedi, Zhanna Marchenko, Mohammed Osama, Esther Prummer, Marina Waldmann**.

**Zhanna Marchenko** is the same analyst who prepared the geo-spatial analytics for DOMA's Moscow restaurant location case (portfolio). This case is published to demonstrate academic methodology and team expertise, **with full authorship preserved and source disclosed** — not as a project delivered by DOMA for a client.

### What this is
A composite sustainability assessment of Kampala, Uganda across 6 domains: urban planning, green infrastructure, mobility, energy, water, and waste. Each domain includes a composite rating (0–5 scale, based on 18 indicators benchmarked internationally), a diagnosis of the current situation, and proposed goals/actions.

### Data
All figures on the page come from the poster authors' working file (`Templates_Poster_Graphs_WS21_v1.xlsx`, dated 08.11.2021) and the final poster (`Poster_20.02.2022_Final_Version.pdf`):
- Demographic and country-level indicators (population, density, income, literacy, life expectancy, ecological footprint)
- Sustainability rating — 18 indicators across 6 domains, 0–5 scale
- Population age/gender structure (2019)
- Green/blue infrastructure (2022)
- Modal split of transportation (2008)
- Primary energy sources (2014)
- Water supply sources (2016)
- Waste disposal methods (2016)

The full list of 10 primary sources is available via the QR code on the original poster (not included in this HTML).

### How to open
`kampala.html` is a self-contained HTML page with an embedded logo and all charts rendered as pure SVG (no external libraries or network dependency). The **EN/RU** switch in the header toggles all text, numeric figures (number format, currency, units), and chart labels.

### Why it's in the portfolio
Demonstrates: (1) a methodology for composite, multi-domain territorial sustainability assessment with quantitative scoring — applicable to other territorial-analytics cases; (2) the qualifications of an analyst (Zhanna Marchenko) who has also worked on DOMA's commercial cases; (3) experience working with international data and standards, beyond the Russian context.
